  1. What even is the terminal and how do I get there?

    • The terminal is a thing on your computer that controlls everything and can let you do extra stuff
    • On Macs, you can press Cmd + Space to open up the Spotlight Search, then you search Terminal

  2. Basic Things You Should Know

    • I'm just gonna hope that you understand the concept of the organization tree because there's no way I'm explaining it here. Your teacher will explain if you don't know.
    • A directory is a place where files are stored. It's like a drawer, section, or folder in a filing cabinet
    • The open line in your terminal should look something like this:
      blah blah user blah blah ~ %
      • The squigle, or tilde, tells you that you are in the home directory.
      • The percent sign tells you that it is ready to take commands.

    • cd

      • Stands for change directory. Pretty self explainatory, it lets you switch directories.

    • pwd

      • Shows you what directory you are currently in. It stands for print working directory

    • ls

      • Lists the contents of the directory, or the things you can cd into.

  3. Other Stuff

    • less

      • Let's you look at the contents of a file

    • cp

      • Copy file or folder , there are more things to add to tell your computer what to copy and where to put it, but am I going to tell you that? No. It's probably not important right now if you just learned whad cd means.

    • mv

      • Move file or folder. Works in a similar way to cp.

    • rm

      • Remove file or folder. Also works like cp.
