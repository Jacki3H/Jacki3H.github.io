Info/Non Traumatic Backstory

No seriously it's not traumatic. But you should see my other recipe

Why is it called wedding soup? This recipe is traditonally Italian, and even though it's called wedding soup, it usually does't have anything to do with weddings. It comes from mistranslating "marriage soup" to "wedding soup", when marriage in this case was refering to the "marriage" of many different ingrediants, not a literal wedding. I think some people do have it at weddings though.

Every other year, for Christmas, my family drives 7 hours to my Grandparents house so we can spend winter break with them and my cousins. I remember that my grandma would always make wedding soup for us, using her and I would be happy to have it after so many hours in the car. She used a recipe that had been with her famly for many generations, and I'll do my best to cover that recipe here.

"We always make wedding soup when the family travels home for the holidays. It makes a warm welcome"

- My Grandma

Tips, Tricks and Substitutions

  • I like this recipe best with really small pasta, unlike in the photo I used at the top. We usually use acini di pepe, which means seeds of pepper. They look like little balls.
  • Grating your own parmesan cheese tastes way better! Do it!
    • If you like to use that kraft "parmesan cheese" that comes out of the shaker container you need to leave this website. Get out.
  • If you're confused by the recipe don't blame me, it's from my grandma. At least I tried to be authentic.

Recipe

Preperation time: 25 mins prep/25 mins cooking Servings: 10? I don't know how much you eat


Ingredients Amount
Lean ground beef 1 lb
Eggs 1
Chopped carrots I don't like cooked carrots, so ideally none but about 1 1/2 cups
Yellow onion 1 1/2 cups
Garlic 4 cloves
Acini di pepe pasta 1 cup
Chicken bullion cubes? One for each cup of water
Chicken breast 1. I think that says 1?
Celery 3/4 cup
Baby spinach, rougly chopped 3/4 cup
Chicken broth 5 cans

  1. Make meatballs with beef, egg, and desired amount of parmesan and parsley flakes
  2. Simmer chicken breats for 20 mintues in enough water to cover
  3. Remove chicken and let it cool
  4. Add 1 chicken bullion cube for each cup of water into the water in the pot
  5. Add carrots, celery, and onion
  6. Add chicken broth
  7. Simmer until vegetables are tender
  8. Shred chicken and return to pot
  9. Let soup come to boil and drop in meatballs
  10. Simmer until meatballs are cooked
  11. Cook pasta until tender in a seperate pot
  12. Drain pasta and add to soup
  13. Enjoy or else
